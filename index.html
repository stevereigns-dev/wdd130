Ghana

Ghana is a vibrant West African nation situated along the Gulf of Guinea, clebrated for its rich history, cultural diversity, and stable democracy. Bordered by Cote d'Ivoire, Burkina Faso, and Togo, it made history in 1957 by becoming the first sub-Saharan African country to achieve independence from colonial rule. Today, the country is home to over 35 million people, with Accra as its bustling capital and kumasi as a major historic and cultural hub. Geographically diverse, Ghana features coastal savannas, lush rainforests, and Lake-Volta, one of the largest artificial reservoirs in the World. Economically. it is driven by agriculture and abundant natural resources, standing as leading global producer of gold and cocoa alongside emerging oil, tech, and service sectors. Renowned for warm hospitality, often embodied by the phrase Akwaaba, Ghana remains an influential cultural and politcal anchor on the African continent
